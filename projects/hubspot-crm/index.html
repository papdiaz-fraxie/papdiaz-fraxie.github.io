HubSpot CRM Data Quality & Governance Lab

A HubSpot CRM portfolio project focused on data quality, record structure, governance and reporting using synthetic member-organisation and contact data.

Context

Applying proven CRM data-quality experience to a HubSpot scenario.

My professional CRM data-quality work has mainly been in Salesforce, including duplicate investigation, account validation, hierarchy checks, QA and reporting. This portfolio scenario applies the same data-quality approach to a synthetic membership CRM structure in HubSpot.

Scenario

A small, realistic CRM environment.

The project uses synthetic member organisations and contacts, with controlled examples of duplicate, incomplete, outdated and incorrectly classified records.

Project scope

What the project covers

The scope mirrors common CRM data-management tasks: intake, structure, quality control and governance.

01 · Data intakeImport and map CRM records

Load synthetic companies and contacts and map fields into a controlled CRM structure.

02 · StructureProperties and associations

Create practical properties and link contacts to the correct organisations.

03 · Data qualityInvestigate record issues

Work through duplicates, missing fields, stale data and incorrect classifications.

04 · GovernanceViews, reporting and standards

Create useful quality views and document naming, review and record-maintenance rules.
