HubSpot CRM Data Quality & Governance Lab

A hands-on HubSpot CRM learning project focused on data quality, record structure, governance and reporting using synthetic member-organisation and contact data.

In progressHands-on HubSpot build not yet completed. This page records the scope and evidence that will be added as the work is completed.
Why this project

Extend proven CRM data-quality experience into HubSpot.

My professional CRM data-quality work has mainly been in Salesforce, including duplicate investigation, account validation, hierarchy checks, QA and reporting. This lab is designed to build practical HubSpot capability around similar problems without presenting the learning project as production experience.

Planned scope

A small, realistic CRM environment.

The project will use synthetic member organisations and contacts, with controlled examples of duplicate, incomplete, outdated and incorrectly classified records.

Planned hands-on work

What will be demonstrated

Each item will only be described as completed after it has actually been performed in HubSpot.

01 · Data intakeImport and map CRM records

Load synthetic companies and contacts and map fields into a controlled CRM structure.

02 · StructureProperties and associations

Create practical properties and link contacts to the correct organisations.

03 · Data qualityInvestigate record issues

Work through duplicates, missing fields, stale data and incorrect classifications.

04 · GovernanceViews, reporting and standards

Create useful quality views and document naming, review and record-maintenance rules.
